Frequently Asked Questions

You can swap pre-approved electives directly from the Major Sheet tab. Go to Course Registration, find the Technical or General Elective slot, and select a replacement course from the dropdown menu.

A time conflict happens when two course sections share the exact same days and hours. The portal's Section Selector will automatically identify and block conflicting options from being chosen.

Academic holds are applied due to pending advisor reviews, financial balances, or library fees. Please view the hold details on your dashboard and contact the Deanship of Admissions to clear your status.
